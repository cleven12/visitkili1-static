FAQ

Questions before you book.

What is the best first Kilimanjaro route?

For most climbers we recommend 7-day Machame or 7-8 day Lemosho because the pacing gives better acclimatization than shorter routes.

Can I combine Kilimanjaro, safari, and Zanzibar?

Yes. A common structure is climb first, safari second, and Zanzibar last so the beach days become recovery rather than pre-climb travel fatigue.

Do you run private trips?

Yes. Private climbs, private safari vehicles, family trips, honeymoon itineraries, and custom dates are all available.

What should I send to get a realistic plan?

Send travel month, group size, fitness level, preferred comfort level, total days, and whether you want mountain, safari, beach, or all three.
