How we operate

A practical team for high-altitude and safari logistics.

Good trips are built before the first trailhead. We confirm route fit, timing, gear, crew, transport, park permits, accommodation, and payment steps before guests commit.

Moshi-based

We live and work at the foot of Kilimanjaro — not booking from overseas.

TALA Licensed

Registered Tanzania tour operator with full licensing and insurance.

Transparent pricing

Deposit, balance, inclusions, exclusions — all visible before you commit.

Route matching

We match guests to Machame, Lemosho, Marangu, Rongai, Northern Circuit, Meru, safari, or combo routes based on fitness, time, season, and budget.

Safety rhythm

Daily pacing, hydration reminders, altitude checks, crew communication, and realistic summit planning are part of the standard.

Safari & combo planning

Serengeti, Ngorongoro, Zanzibar beach — all coordinated from the same Moshi base after summit day.
